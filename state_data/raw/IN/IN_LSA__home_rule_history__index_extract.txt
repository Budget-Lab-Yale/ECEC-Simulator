Sec. 9. Tests of Submeters Prior to Installation. No submeter shall be placed in service unless its accuracy has been established.
If any submeter is removed from actual service and replaced by another submeter for any purpose whatsoever, it shall be properly
tested and adjusted before being placed in service again. (Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission; 170 IAC 4-5-9; filed Dec 13, 1984,
