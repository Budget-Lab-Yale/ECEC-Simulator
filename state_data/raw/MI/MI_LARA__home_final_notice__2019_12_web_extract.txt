L124@P2: R 400.1902 Applicant; licensee; requirements.
L125@P2:  Rule 2. (1) An applicant and a licensee shall meet all of the following requirements:
L126@P2-3:  (a) Be 18 years of age or older.4

L128@P3: approved training track and hours for child care home providers through MiRegistry.
L129@P3:  (c) Permanently reside in the child care home as a member of the household.
L130@P3:  (d) Have proof of certification in both of the following:
L131@P3:  (i) Infant, child, and adult cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR).
L132@P3:  (ii) First aid.
L133@P3:  (e) Have documentation of completed training in both of the following:
L134@P3:  (i) Recognition and reporting of child abuse and neglect.
L135@P3:  (ii) Prevention and control of infectious disease, including immunizations.
L136@P3:  (f) Attend an orientation provided by the department.
L137@P3:  (2) An applicant or licensee shall be of responsible character and shall be suitable and able
L138@P3: to meet the needs of children and provide for their care, supervision, and protection.
L139@P3:  (3) All persons, including minors, residing in the child care home shall meet all of the
L140@P3: following requirements:
L141@P3:  (a) Be suitable to meet the needs of children.
L142@P3:  (b) Be able to ensure that services and facilities are conducive to the welfare of children.
L143@P3:  (c) Act in a manner that is conducive to the welfare of children.
L144@P3:  (d) Demonstrate a willingness and ability to comply with the act and these rules.
