(i) A child care facility shall prevent exposure of children to individuals, animals, and

(1) document permission from the parent for a school-age child to participate in 
activities and visit friends off premises;  
(2) obtain permission from the parent for a child to participate in moderate risk 
activities, such as swimming, walks to the beach, or vehicle trips; and  
(3) arrange for the use of appropriate safety equipment, including a well-fitting 
helmet for activities such as rollerblading or bicycle, skateboard, or scooter riding.

7 AAC 57.510. Maximum group size in child care centers.  (a)  A child care center 
shall organize its learning environment so that children may participate in activities individually 
and in small groups, so that the development of each child is supported.   
 
(b)  A child care center shall provide environmental cues to children to indicate an 
interest or activity center and to facilitate a limited number of children at the interest or activity 
center. Environmental cues include the use of visible barriers, partitions, colored rugs, and tables 
with a limited number of chairs.
