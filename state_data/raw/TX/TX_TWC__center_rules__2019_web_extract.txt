CCL Minimum Standards  
Division 2, Classroom Ratios and Group Sizes for Centers Licensed to Care for 13 or More Children  
§746.1601 How many children may one caregiver supervise?  
The classroom ratio is the number of children one caregiver may supervise and is shown in the following chart. 
The classroom ratio is based on the specified age of the children in the group, unless otherwise stated in this 
subchapter: 
If the specified age of the children in the group is…  
Then the maximum number of children one 
caregiver may supervise is…  
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 0 – 11 months | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 4
--- | ---
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 12 – 17 months | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 5
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 18 – 23 months | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 9
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 2 years | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 11
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 3 years | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 15
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 4 years | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 18
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 5 years | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 22
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 6-8 years | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 26
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 9-13 years | Then the maximum number of children one caregiver may supervise is… 26
 
§746.1609 What is the maximum group size?  
The maximum group size and the number of children two or more caregivers may supervise when 13 or more 
children are in care is specified in the following chart and is based on the specified age of the children in the 
group: 
 
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 0 – 11 months | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 10
--- | ---
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 12 – 17 months | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 13
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 18 – 23 months | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 18
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 2 years | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 22
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 3 years | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 30
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 4 years | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 35
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 5 years | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 35
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 6-8 years | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 35
If the specified age of the children in the group is… 9 – 13 years | Then the maximum group size and number of children two or more caregivers may supervise is… 35
