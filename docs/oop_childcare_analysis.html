Two ways to read out-of-pocket. Net of subsidy counts only demand-side subsidies (nonzero in Universal and Means-tested), leaving the childcare tax credit aside. Net of subsidy + CDCTC additionally credits the Child & Dependent Care Tax Credit, allocated to each child from the family's realized credit in proportion to capped, net-of-subsidy qualifying expense ($3k/child).

The ARPA line depends on the basis. Net of subsidy only, ARPA sits slightly above baseline (the expanded credit lifts demand and equilibrium prices but isn't counted here). Switch to net of subsidy + CDCTC and ARPA drops well below baseline. The credit's real value erodes over time — the $3k cap and dollar thresholds are not inflation-indexed — so the gap to baseline narrows toward 2055.
